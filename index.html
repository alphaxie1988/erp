Rates: LTA ERP rates for passenger cars effective 7 September 2026, via LTA DataMall (as compiled by MoreBetter / Liddat from the official table). Motorcycle = ½ car rate, heavy goods / small bus = 1.5×, very heavy / big bus = 2×. ERP charges weekdays only — no charge Sat, Sun or public holidays; on the eve of major holidays ERP ends at 1pm. A bracket like 07:30–08:00 means from 07:30 inclusive until 08:00 exclusive. LTA reviews rates quarterly, so check OneMotoring after the next review.
